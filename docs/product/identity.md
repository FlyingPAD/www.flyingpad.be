# Flying PAD Product Identity

Flying PAD is the public-facing learning, creation and transmission branch of The Ninth Pad.

It should remain useful and credible even for someone who never discovers the deeper shared mythology.

## Scope

Flying PAD can contain:
- music;
- acoustics and musicology;
- audio production;
- composition and covers;
- video production;
- AI creative tools;
- code and interactive tools;
- interviews;
- production diaries;
- creative process;
- learning paths.

## Guardian expression

Flying PAD expresses the **Guardian-side** creative psychology:
- observe;
- understand;
- contextualize;
- preserve;
- transmit;
- accept limits without giving up curiosity.

This does not make Guardian morally superior to Hunter. Shared polarity belongs to The Ninth Pad; this file owns its Flying PAD product expression.

## Public separation

Flying PAD and HHH should each remain independently coherent.

Connections can be discovered through recurring philosophy, colors, sounds, symbols and structures rather than direct “secret shared universe” exposition.
