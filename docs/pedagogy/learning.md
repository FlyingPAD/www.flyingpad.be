# Learning & Pedagogy

Flying PAD applies the shared Ninth Pad learning sequence directly:

> **Reproduire → S’approprier → Créer → Comprendre**

The canonical philosophy is owned by `FlyingPAD/the-ninth-pad`. This document owns its **pedagogical application**.

## Reproduire

Let the learner do something real early.

Reproduction gives embodied structural experience before total theoretical understanding.

## S’approprier

Help the learner adapt the reproduced pattern, vary it and connect it to prior knowledge until it becomes usable rather than memorized.

## Créer

Move from exercise to personal output.

Creation exposes gaps and turns borrowed structure into active knowledge.

## Comprendre

Use theory, comparison, failure and reflection to reorganize the experience afterward.

Understanding can become extremely precise without pretending to be a final universal 100%.

## Teaching rule

> **Do before demanding full understanding.**

Theory is valuable because it organizes experience; it should not become a gate that prevents experimentation.

## Tone

Flying PAD should be:
- accessible;
- generous;
- practical;
- curious;
- technically credible;
- philosophical without becoming pompous;
- comfortable saying that a model is useful without claiming it is reality itself.
