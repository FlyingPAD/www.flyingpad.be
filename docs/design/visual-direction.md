# Flying PAD Visual Direction

## Shared foundation

**Colors are alive.**

The shared principle comes from The Ninth Pad. Flying PAD expresses it differently from HHH.

## Guardian-side expression

Prefer:
- measured living color;
- breathing motion;
- clarity and observation;
- harmony;
- enough richness to feel alive without competing layers attacking at once;
- technically legible educational/creative surfaces.

This is not generic minimalism and not moral coding.

HHH may express the same foundation as controlled maximalism / rigid structure, living excess. Flying PAD should feel calmer, more contemplative and transmissive.

## Product rule

The interface should support learning and creation first. Mythology may enrich the experience, but should never obstruct a visitor who simply came to learn or use a tool.
