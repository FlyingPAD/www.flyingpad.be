## 1. Identity

Flying PAD is the public-facing learning, creation and transmission side of the wider universe.

It can cover:

- music;
- acoustics and musicology;
- audio production;
- composition and covers;
- video production;
- AI creative tools;
- code and interactive tools;
- interviews;
- production diaries;
- creative process;
- learning paths.

It should remain credible even for a visitor who never discovers the hidden mythology.

## 2. The Guardian

The Guardian is the internal archetype of this side.

He does not seek to own the Pads. He observes, understands, contextualizes, preserves and transmits. He accepts limits more readily than The Hunter and tends toward inclination/consent/continuity rather than forcing a thing into another form.

Where The Hunter asks “what can I make this become?”, the Guardian more often asks “what is already here, what does it mean, and how can I help someone understand it?”

This does not make the Guardian morally superior. His strength is care and transmission; his shadow is passivity or excessive consensus: understanding/preserving can become an excuse never to act or transform.

## 3. Tone

Flying PAD should be:

- accessible;
- generous;
- thoughtful;
- practical;
- curious;
- technically credible;
- philosophical without becoming pompous;
- comfortable admitting that a model/theory is useful without pretending it is absolute truth.

A useful recurring idea from the source material is:

> The map is not the territory.

Human theory organizes perception; it does not become reality merely because it is useful.

## 3a. Guardian visual psychology

**CURRENT creative direction:** Flying PAD shares the foundational rule **Colors are alive**, but its expression differs from HHH.

Where HHH/Hunter uses controlled maximalism, Flying PAD/Guardian should feel more measured, harmonious and contemplative:
- color remains active rather than dead/static;
- motion breathes instead of attacking;
- visual systems favor clarity, observation and transmission;
- effects can still be rich, but fewer layers compete at once;
- the interface should feel alive without feeling acquisitive or predatory.

The contrast is creative psychology, not moral coding.

## 4. The Source and knowledge

The Source is the deeper mythic origin before fixed categories.

Flying PAD can use the Source metaphor to frame learning:

- humans choose reference systems;
- music theory is an organized map of sound, not sound itself;
- craft matters precisely because maps are useful;
- learning a system and understanding its limits are compatible.

Do not turn every tutorial into heavy lore exposition. Mythology should enrich pedagogy, not obstruct it.

### 4a. Learning sequence

**CANON — Flying PAD pedagogy.** The preferred learning order is:

> **Reproduire → S’approprier → Créer → Comprendre**

This ordering is deliberate rather than rhetorical. Learners first reproduce a working thing closely enough to feel its structure, then internalize and adapt it, then create with it, and only afterward synthesize the deeper understanding made possible by comparison, failure, variation and intent.

`S’approprier` means internalizing knowledge until it can be used naturally and transformed personally; it does not mean claiming ownership of another person's work.

Understanding is never framed as a final possession. Flying PAD should be comfortable with **99.9% / 99.9999% understanding**: a model may become extraordinarily useful and precise while still remaining conditioned by viewpoint, context and incomplete experience. This pedagogical asymptote deliberately echoes White.

The practical implication is important: Flying PAD should **let people do before demanding that they fully understand**. Explanation follows experience and helps reorganize it; it should not become a gate that prevents experimentation.

## 5. White / Flying Pad

The White Pad represents perfection as an unreachable/stabilization-resistant ideal. A materialized idea acquires constraints and point of view, so no concrete result can be guaranteed as universally perfect.

The current conceptual representation of White is a **pure white screen plus absolute silence**: not a depiction of perfection, but an intentional absence that lets each observer project something different into it.

In the Flying PAD visual logic, White escapes while the remaining Pads are not imprisoned. The Guardian accepts the absence, studies what remains and understands that the missing answer cannot be permanently fixed.

This contrasts with HHH, where The Hunter keeps intervening and creating new variations in pursuit of that same impossible ideal.

## 6. Relationship to HHH

**CANON — public separation.**

Flying PAD does not directly advertise HHH as its adult/hidden counterpart, and HHH does not explain Flying PAD. At the private creative level, **Flying PAD expresses the Guardian side while HHH expresses the Hunter side**: two approaches to creation around the same White ideal, not two projects that must explicitly answer each other on the surface.

Connections may appear through:

- colors;
- nine-position imagery with an absence;
- White leaving;
- Source vocabulary;
- shared sound motifs;
- recurring philosophical questions;
- subtle visual structures;
- the contrast between observation and capture.

The audience should first experience two coherent projects. Recognition of the relationship is a reward for attention.

At the internal creative level, both projects belong to the wider **The Ninth Pad** mythos. That name is the shared creative umbrella, not currently an instruction to expose a direct cross-brand connection or to treat it as an indexed HHH Lore.

Cross-project understanding should work like the learning philosophy itself: the audience first encounters and reproduces patterns, then makes them their own through recognition, then forms new interpretations, and only later understands how the pieces connect.

## 7. Cross-media role

Flying PAD is broad enough to contain the creator's public creative output without forcing every work into one genre.

The Pad language can annotate or reinterpret existing work later:

- a song may have dominant colors;
- a tutorial may discuss how humans organize perception;
- an interview may naturally explore creative intent;
- a production diary may show the craft behind manifestation;
- an interactive tool may visualize relationships without presenting the mythology as literal science.
